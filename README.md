# My Favorite Movie: Teen Wolf

> *"It's not about the wolf. It's about what you do with the power you're given."*

## About the Movie
**Teen Wolf** is a supernatural comedy-drama movie about **Scott Howard**, an ordinary high school teenager who discovers that he is a werewolf. Instead of hiding it, he uses his abilities to help his school basketball team win and learns to accept himself for who he really is.

## Why I Like It
* **Fun Story:** Mixes high school life, basketball, and werewolf powers.
* **Great Friendship:** Scott's best friend **Stiles** is loyal and hilarious.
* **Good Message:** Shows that staying true to yourself matters more than popularity.

## Main Characters
1. **Scott Howard** – The main character who discovers his werewolf abilities.
2. **Stiles** – Scott's best friend and biggest supporter.
3. **Harold Howard** – Scott's father who also shares the werewolf secret.
4. **Boof** – Scott's close friend who likes him for who he really is.
